HARDBALL — Cap-Era Moneyball

Executive one-pager · Prepared for the December 2026 Winter Meetings · Generated 2026-11-02 21:23 UTC from committed repo snapshots by src/make_onepager.py · Real data only; no invented numbers.

Thesis: under a hard cap, buy the persistent inputs — contact and walk discipline — and control-walking arms. Do not pay for noisy power output.

Why the edge is real

  • Skills persist; outputs do not. 2025→2026, 80 players (200+ PA): contact r = 0.899, walk rate r = 0.655, OBP r = 0.298. Buying contact/walk skill buys something that carries.
  • Our projections sit on the public benchmark. Marcel vs FanGraphs Steamer 2027, ID-matched (never by name): OBP r = 0.889, n = 201, mean |diff| = 0.010.
  • Winning cheap is observable, not theoretical. 2026:

Cap reality (owners’ proposed 2027 numbers)

  • Cap $245.3M / floor $171.2M / 15% per-player max. Proposed, not law — CBA expires Dec 1, 2026.
  • 7 teams over the cap and must cut — most: NYM ($101.4M over).
  • 15 teams under the floor and must add — most: CLE ($96.7M to add). The disciplined winners are the ones the system now forces to spend.

The honesty box — what we do NOT claim

  • Team-wins forecasting is weak (MAE ~9–13 wins, barely better than the league average). The earlier R² 0.80 team-wins claim was retracted (Cycles 18–19). We sell player-level projections + cap rosters, not a wins formula.
  • *Availability stress is a level tax, not a rescue. Uniform stress from 201 real players’ 2025–26 IL records (mean availability 0.912): every budget loses ~4–5 WAR; the floor/cap ratio is unchanged. Per-player factors cover only 12 of 33 rostered players, so they are not applied selectively (that would penalize the best-measured players).
  • Salaries cover 24 of 30 teams. Spotrac blocked ARI, BOS, CHC, MIL, TB, TEX (927 rows, 864 ID-matched). The gap is labeled in the data, not filled.
  • WAR is a public stat (FanGraphs fWAR / Baseball-Reference bWAR, free) but the two formulas disagree and the MLB API does not return it; we surface its reproducible inputs (wOBA, wRC+, FIP, K-BB%) rather than inventing a single WAR.
