# HARDBALL — Meeting Talk-Track (10 minutes, Figure-by-Figure)
*For the GP to present to an owner. Every number is real and measured; every
honesty caveat is already inside the pitch so it survives a skeptical room.*

**Weapons:** the dashboard, opened on Figure 1. This page tells you what to
say and in what order. Don't read it — talk it.

---

## HOWDY (0:30)
"The owners want a hard cap. So the question isn't 'who spends the most' —
that game is over. The question is: **which team deploys a constrained budget
better than the teams it competes against?** And the answer is measurable."

## THE EDGE — Figure 1 (2:00)
Point at the bars. "Here's the single most important chart in the room.
We tracked real players year over year. What a player *does* that's a *skill*
— making contact, drawing walks — **carries. In every one of three season
pairs, contact rate persists at r ≈ 0.8-0.9.** But the *output* owners are
tempted to buy — on-base percentage — is mostly luck, and it does **not**
carry (r ≈ 0.3)."

**The line:** "You don't pay for the OBP. You pay for the skill that produces
it. That's a measurable edge."

## WHY THIS ISN'T A GAMBLE — Figures 2-3 (1:30)
Fig 2 (run diff): "Better teams score more than they allow — that's real,
but it describes *this* season; it doesn't predict next."
Fig 3 (payroll vs wins): "Money buys some wins. But the winning-cheap teams —
Milwaukee, Tampa, Cleveland — win ~95 at a third to a quarter the payroll.
**They're the ones who've discovered the edge we're showing you.**"

## HOW THE WINNERS ARE BUILT — Figures 4-8 (2:30)
Quick sweep: "Winning-cheap teams dominate contact, OBP, and walks (Fig 4);
they field control-walking arms (Fig 8); their health is durable (Fig 9).
The 2027 roster (Fig 10) is built from real projections on exactly those
inputs." Don't linger — the charts carry it.

## THE CAP IS THE OPPORTUNITY — Figures 12-13 (2:00)
Fig 12 (value metrics): "This is what we compute instead of faking WAR —
wOBA, wRC+, FIP. Real, reproducible."
Fig 13 (cap): "Here's the kicker. These seven teams are over the cap and MUST
cut — New York $101M, Los Angeles $94M. And these fifteen — **including
Cleveland (+$97M) and Tampa (+$76M)** — are under the floor and forced to add.
The cap makes the rich cut; the floor hands the disciplined winners real money
to deploy. **The system is now engineered to reward exactly the teams that
waste the least.**"

## HONESTY — what we will NOT claim (1:00)
"This is where we're different. We do **not** tell you we can predict team
wins — forecasting is genuinely weak (about 9-13 wins off), and saying
otherwise would be a lie that gets exposed. WAR is proprietary and absent from
public data — we surface its real inputs instead. What we *can* show, measured
over three years: skills persist, luck doesn't. **That's the edge you can act
on.**"

## ASK (0:30)
"Give us your cap-constrained team and we'll show you which contact/walk
players and control arms to buy." — close.
